# How Well can Claude Opus 5.5 Imitate a 19th Century Human Author?
_Stylometric Analysis of over 580+ human and AI generated texts (~3,000 words per excerpt)_
By Harshini Sre P and Vrishodharan K. Swami

# CORPUS + Details
## AI Generations - 126 * 3 Generations
2,500 - 3,000 word excerpts generated by **Claude Opus 5.5**

**Across 3 Prompting Styles:**
_"Imitate the style of the author"_
1) **Zero Shot Prompting** - No examples were given as examples for Claude to model style off
2) **One Shot Prompting**- One excerpt were given as examples for Claude to model style off
3) **Multi Shot Prompting**- Three excerpts were given as examples for Claude to model style off

**3 Topics Per Prompting Style:**
_"Depict the following scene []"_
1) A tense conversation between a couple
2) Cousins catching up after a long time apart
3) A parent reprimanding a young child

14 authors * 3 prompting styles * 3 topics per prompting style = **126 TEXTS**

## Human Texts - 210
14 authors * 3 novels each * 5 texts per novel = 210 Total Texts
2,500 - 3,000 words each

_**(Three Generations with the same prompts were taken to repeat the study for generalizability)**_

# Metrics + Methods

## Metrics
1) Lexical Entropy
2) Sentence Length Deviation (Burstiness)
3) Most Frequently Used Words (Burrow's Delta)
4) Character N-Grams
5) POS N-Grams
6) Punctuation

## Methods
1) **Support Vector Machine**
We trained a Support Vector Machine on human texts and tested them on each AI text. We checked to see which one of the fourteen classes the AI texts fell into and measured the F1 & Accuracy Score.
(To check whether or not the SVM was able to put an imitated AI text into the class with the author it imitated)

2. **Cluster Diagrams**
While the SVM was forced to choose one of the 14 human author classes for the AI texts, a cluster diagram measured whether an AI text was clearly different from the human texts.
(2 clusters, Human + AI)
For all 3 generations we noticed that at 300MFW, AI texts were classified seperately from humans, indicating that AI contains a distinct style.

3. **Bar Graphs**
To show differences in punctuation, lexical entropy and sentence length deviation to better understand how AI differs from human writing.
